Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 3, 2026

1. Introduction

Techsavyy (“we”, “us” or “our”) provides a collection of websites, applications and digital tools for learning, productivity, personal finance, utilities and other everyday needs. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, disclosed and protected when you visit techsavyy.com or use a Techsavyy service.

Individual products may display additional privacy notices describing product-specific data practices. If a product-specific notice conflicts with this policy, the product-specific notice applies to that product.

2. Information we may collect

  • Information you provide: such as your name, email address, account details, messages, feedback and information you choose to enter into an application.
  • Authentication information: when you choose to sign in through a third-party provider such as Google, we receive the information that provider makes available and that you authorize, which may include your name, email address and profile image. We do not receive your Google password.
  • Usage and device information: such as pages or features used, approximate usage times, browser type, device information, diagnostic events and log data.
  • Cookies and similar technologies: which may be used for essential site functionality, preferences, security and analytics where enabled.

3. How we use information

We may use information to provide and operate our services, authenticate users, save preferences and user-created content, respond to support requests, maintain security, diagnose errors, understand product usage, improve features and comply with applicable legal obligations.

4. Google and other third-party services

If you use Google sign-in or connect a Google service, the relevant Google account information is handled in accordance with the permissions shown during authorization. Our use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will comply with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. You can review or revoke third-party access in your Google Account settings.

Our services may rely on third-party providers for hosting, authentication, analytics, storage and infrastructure. Those providers process information as needed to provide their services under their own terms and privacy policies.

5. Sharing and disclosure

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information with service providers who help us operate our services, when you ask us to share it, to protect users or our services, or when disclosure is required by law. We do not use information for purposes materially different from those described here without providing an appropriate notice where required.

6. Data retention and security

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the relevant service, meet legitimate operational needs, resolve disputes and comply with legal obligations. Retention periods can vary by product and by the type of information. We use reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect information, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

7. Your choices and rights

You may be able to access, correct or delete information through the relevant product settings. You can also contact us to request access, correction or deletion, or to ask a question about our handling of your information. Some information may need to be retained for legal, security or essential operational reasons. You can withdraw consent for optional third-party account connections by revoking access with that provider.

8. Children's privacy

Some Techsavyy products may be designed for learning or family use. Those products should be used with appropriate parent or guardian involvement. We do not knowingly collect personal information from a child in a manner that is prohibited by applicable law. A parent or guardian who believes a child has provided personal information may contact us so we can review the request.

9. International processing

Depending on the service providers used, information may be processed or stored in countries other than the country where you live. Where required, we take steps intended to provide appropriate safeguards for such processing.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as our services or legal requirements change. The updated version will be published on this page with a revised effective date.

11. Contact us

For privacy questions or requests, contact support@techsavyy.com.
