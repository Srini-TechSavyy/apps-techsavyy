About Techsavyy

Ideas become things.

Techsavyy is a collection of small tools, experiments, and apps built from curiosity and shared along the way — things designed to be useful, simple, or just fun to use.

Small apps, useful ideas

The goal is simple: build focused apps that solve a problem without adding unnecessary complexity. Some are for learning, some for everyday tasks, and some exist simply because the idea was interesting enough to build.

Always evolving

Techsavyy is an ongoing collection rather than a single product. New apps and experiments are added over time, while existing ones continue to improve based on how people use them.
